The app now learns about the surface independently of whether the character fits at the exact point you are aiming at.

The important change: while the center still hits a valid surface, an oversized footprint near its detected edge no longer erases surface verification progress.

What “progress” means

Evidence, not a percentage.

The app remembers which plane it is observing, its height, the starting camera position, and when observation started.

It records whether the phone has moved at least 8 cm from that starting position in the horizontal plane. Rotating the phone or moving it only vertically does not count.

After 1.5 seconds of continuous valid observation, that movement evidence can verify the surface. The two conditions can accumulate together.

These are internal checks. The app does not currently show a percentage bar. Actual readiness may take longer because target stability and other checks also need to pass.

What still starts over

Lost evidence still resets.

  • The center loses its valid surface hit, leaves the allowed range, or no longer aims sufficiently downward.
  • The detected plane changes, its height shifts by more than 2 cm, or the observation gap exceeds 200 ms.
  • Measured depth conflicts with the plane.

The fix covers footprint-fit failures while a valid center hit continues. It does not preserve progress through tracking loss or every boundary crossing.

Example / Your tabletop

What you should experience now

01 / FOUNDAim at the table.

The center hits its detected surface. Surface observation begins.

02 / NEAR EDGEScan sideways.

The footprint may extend beyond the detected patch. The amber marker remains visible, and valid surface observation continues.

03 / ROOM TO STANDAim farther inward.

The footprint fits. Previously collected surface evidence is available; the new target must still settle.

04 / READYHold steady, then place.

Once both checks and the remaining gates pass, the marker turns green and the button is enabled.

Target settling: movement more than 4 cm from its reference position restarts the target check. The marker is circular, but the current fit test still checks the four corners of its enclosing square.

Automatic placement: outside move mode, supported depth additionally needs matching, settled depth evidence (350 ms and at least 10 frames). The manual button can be ready sooner. Missing depth alone does not block manual placement; conflicting depth does.
