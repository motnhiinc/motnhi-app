Five marketing panels, each 430×932 CSS px. Run tools/export-appstore-shots.sh to write build/appstore/screenshot-1…5.png at 3× (1290×2796, the 6.7″/6.9″ iPhone slot).

Phone screens are drawn from app-screens-wireframes.html; colour and type come from StitchTokens. UI copy is the real app_en.arb / app_vi.arb values. Headlines are marketing copy — edit them in the .head blocks.

Story content stays English in both sets. Every bundled story is language_code: "en"; there is no Vietnamese catalogue yet, so translating titles and page text here would advertise content the app cannot show.
